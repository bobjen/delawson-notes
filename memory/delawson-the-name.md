**George DeLawson Lowry**, b. 17 January 1880 in Hagersville, Ontario, d. 1919
in Buffalo, New York. Bob's great-grandfather. A piano teacher who played jazz
in the style of Gershwin; married his student Ethel Mabel Foller in 1909.
Page: https://burtleburtle.net/gene/glowry18800117.html

**He went by "Laws", short for DeLawson.**

**How to apply:** When Bob writes or says **"Laws", he means DeLawson** — the
project — not anything else. The nickname is also why the earlier, contaminated
sibling project carried that name; do not let the shared nickname tempt you
toward it (see [[delawson-clean-room]]). The clean-room guard's patterns are
narrow enough (`bobjen/laws`, `laws-notes.git`) that the bare word "Laws" does
not trip them.

Bob learned on 2026-08-14 that the actual cause of death was Landry's
Paralysis, which the page does not yet say; he is updating that page himself.
Not ours to edit.
